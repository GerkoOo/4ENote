4ENote privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

4ENote is an open-source handwriting notes app. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. This page explains exactly what happens with your data.

Your notes stay with you

  • Your notes, drawings, pictures, scans and PDFs are stored on your device.
  • If you turn on cloud sync, they are also stored in your own cloud storage (for example a "4ENote" folder in your own Google Drive), sent directly from your device. They never pass through, and are never stored on, any server run by the 4ENote developers.
  • The developers cannot see, read or access your notes or your cloud storage.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google:

  • 4ENote asks Google for access to only the files 4ENote creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • It also receives your email address, only to show which account is signed in.
  • The sign-in tokens are kept only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • 4ENote uses this access only to save and read your own notes in your own Drive. It does not share, sell or transfer any of it, and does not use it for advertising or any other purpose.

4ENote's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove 4ENote's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the "4ENote" folder from your Drive.

Recognition models

Handwriting-to-text and maths-to-LaTeX run on your device. The first time you use them, the open-source models are downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). That download works like visiting a website (Hugging Face sees your IP address); none of your notes or handwriting is ever sent anywhere for recognition.

Checking for updates

Only when you tap Check for updates, 4ENote asks GitHub (github.com) which version is the newest. That works like visiting a website (GitHub sees your IP address); nothing about you or your notes is sent. 4ENote never checks by itself.

Backups

Back up all notes writes your notes, pictures and folders into one file, saved wherever you choose. It stays under your control; 4ENote sends it nowhere.

Camera

The document scanner uses your camera only while it is open. Photos are processed on your device and only end up in the note you insert them into.

What 4ENote does not do

  • No user accounts or passwords of its own
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising
  • No selling or sharing of any data
  • No third-party services other than the cloud storage you choose, the one-time model downloads and the update check you ask for

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling 4ENote deletes everything it stored on your device.
  • Notes synced to your cloud stay there until you delete the "4ENote" folder.

Source code

4ENote is open source under the GPL-3.0, so all of the above can be checked in the code: github.com/GerkoOo/4ENote.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/GerkoOo/4ENote/issues.
