4ENote

A free, open-source handwriting notes app for Android tablets. Write with a pen, scan and import documents, and keep your notes in your own Google Drive. No ads, no tracking, no accounts.

Download the app (APK) Source code Privacy policy

What it does

  • Pen, ink pen, calligraphy and highlighter; eraser, lasso, layers; scribble or strike out to erase
  • Pinch to zoom and twist to turn a page while writing; full-screen writing
  • Handwriting to editable text, and handwritten maths to LaTeX, on the device
  • Import PDFs and pictures, scan documents with the camera, export and share as PDF
  • Photo frames: ask for a photo on the tablet, take it on your phone, and it lands in the frame
  • Focus timer (Pomodoro) bar
  • Optional sync with your own Google Drive, with a PDF copy of every note

Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, 4ENote asks only for access to the files it creates itself in a "4ENote" folder in your Drive. It cannot see your other files. Your notes travel directly between your device and your Drive; there is no 4ENote server. Read the privacy policy for details.
